Privacy Policy

HundredTap

Last updated: October 3, 2026

HundredTap is made by Pairbeam. We built it to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. HundredTap has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive data from the app.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, stats, streaks, settings and continue balance are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Game Center data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

HundredTap is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page. Questions: berkay@pairbeam.app.
