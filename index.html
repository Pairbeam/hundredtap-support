FAQ

How is the score calculated?
We measure the time between your tap and the moment the needle crosses the line. Within 1 millisecond is 100.0%; every millisecond beyond that costs a little.

Why didn't my streak count on the leaderboard?
The part of a run played after a continue doesn't count for the leaderboard, so rankings can't be bought. The streak you reached before continuing does count.

I bought something but don't see it.
Open the Shop (chart icon → Shop) and tap Restore Purchases. Continues are added to your balance on the device you bought them on.

When does the Daily reset?
Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone in the world plays the same Daily.
